## Artificial Intelligence in Sport: A Narrative Review of Applications, Challenges and Future Trends

URL: ```https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02640414.2025.2518694#d1e297```

Uses: @Zhou2025_AIinSportNarrativeReview

- Zhou et al. (2025) provide a comprehensive narrative review of artificial intelligence applications in sports science, emphasizing how AI technologies are transforming performance analysis, athlete monitoring, and coaching feedback. The authors discuss key methods such as machine learning, computer vision, and predictive analytics while also highlighting challenges like data bias, interpretability, and ethical deployment. This article is important to my project because it establishes the broader research context for using AI in athletic improvement and personalized training systems. It helps justify the design of CaddySense as an AI-based tool that leverages data-driven feedback to enhance player performance, aligning with current research trends in intelligent coaching technologies.
